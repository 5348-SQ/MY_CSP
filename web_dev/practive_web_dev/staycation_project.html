You Should visit Goblin Vally State Park!


Well well well, Goblin Vally is a state park in Utah characterized by looking like mars. This comes from the numerus sandstone goblins, which are mushroom-shaped rock formations (see picture 1) and the red/orange-ish colored, rocky landscape (hover over picture 1). Additonaly, its only a three hour drive from the provo airport making it mildly accessible.
